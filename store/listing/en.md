## Description (4000)
Madame Poly is your AI language tutor, and you talk to her out loud.

Press the button and speak. She hears you, answers in the language you are learning and quietly notes every mistake. When the conversation ends, the mistakes become exercises, and the exercises become a copybook you can print.

EIGHT LANGUAGES, SIX LEVELS
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese from Portugal and from Brazil, and Ukrainian, from A1 to C2. Your level is remembered for each language. Ukrainian and Brazilian Portuguese run as one continuous course. For English, choose British, American or Cockney. The app and every explanation speak your language: English, Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese or Korean.

VOICE, NOT TYPING
Speak and hear the answer. Auto mode catches the pause in your speech; turn it off and you decide when a phrase ends, so slow speech is never cut off.

EVERY MISTAKE EXPLAINED
Under your own words: what you said and what it should be. Tap the question mark for the rule and a full breakdown in your language. Nothing interrupts the conversation.

HOMEWORK FROM YOUR MISTAKES
Five to ten exercises built from what you actually got wrong: fill the gap, fix the sentence, translate. Always in new sentences, so you learn the rule, not the answer.

A COPYBOOK YOU CAN PRINT
Every exercise ever built, grouped by lesson, in one PDF with ruled lines and the answers on a separate page. Print it, or write on a tablet with a stylus.

LISTENING
A passage for your level, read aloud. Six questions: pick the answer, type it or say it out loud. The text stays hidden until you have answered.

WRITING
Ask for a short task, write a few lines and get your text back corrected, every slip explained and turned into exercises.

WORDS AND FLASHCARDS
A vocabulary sheet for your level and topic, with transcription and her voice for every word. Flashcards, a quiz, and spaced review from ten minutes to two months, with gentle reminders.

GRAMMAR, UNIT BY UNIT
A syllabus laid out like a grammar book. Open a unit and she writes the rule with examples, a table, typical mistakes and ten exercises.

MOCK SPEAKING EXAM
The oral part at B1 or B2, in the style of the usual exam for each language: interview, long turn, discussion. No corrections along the way, a full review at the end.

34 TOPICS PER LANGUAGE
Thirty conversation topics and four role-plays: the bank, the pharmacy, the doctor and the repair service. Or set your own subject.

ON EVERY DEVICE
Without an account everything stays on your device. Sign in with email and your progress follows you. Light and dark themes, three text sizes, layouts for phones and tablets.

PLANS
Free: one short conversation (8 replies) or one exam a day, plus a small monthly allowance for listening, writing, words and grammar.
Pro: no daily limit, a monthly allowance of about 20–30 conversations.
Max: about 35–45 conversations a month and a choice of four natural voices.
Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Manage or cancel them any time in your store account settings.
