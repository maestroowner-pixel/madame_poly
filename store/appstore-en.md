## Description
A tutor you talk to out loud.

Press the button and speak. Madame Poly hears you, answers in the language you are learning, and quietly notes every mistake. At the end of the conversation those mistakes become exercises — and the exercises become a copybook you can print and write in with a stylus.

VOICE, NOT TYPING
Speak and hear the answer. No keyboard in the way: the point is to get your tongue moving, not your thumbs. Auto mode catches the pause in your speech itself; turn it off and you mark the end of each phrase yourself, so slow speech is never cut off.

EVERY MISTAKE EXPLAINED
A correction appears under your own words: what you said and what it should be. Tap the question mark for the rule and a full breakdown, written in your language. Nothing interrupts the conversation — you read them when you choose.

HOMEWORK FROM YOUR OWN MISTAKES
When the conversation ends, the app builds five to ten exercises from what you actually got wrong: fill the gap, fix the sentence, translate. The sentence from the conversation is never reused — the same rule comes back in a new one, so you learn the rule rather than memorise the answer.

A COPYBOOK YOU CAN PRINT
Every set of exercises ever built, grouped by lesson. Hold to pick several and they all go into a single PDF: continuous numbering, the rule and the hint above each task, two ruled lines to write on, and the answers on a page of their own. Print it, or open it on a tablet and write with a stylus.

LISTENING, THREE WAYS TO ANSWER
A short passage written for your CEFR level and read aloud — slower at A1, at native pace by C2. Six questions: two to pick from a list, two to type, two to answer out loud. The text stays hidden until you have answered, otherwise it is reading practice, not listening.

FOUR LANGUAGES, SIX LEVELS EACH
English, German, French and Spanish, A1 to C2, remembered separately for each language. For English you also choose the variety: British, American, or Cockney — she answers in dialect but still corrects you toward standard English.

34 TOPICS PER LANGUAGE
Thirty conversation topics plus four role-plays: the bank, the pharmacy, the doctor with a cold, and the appliance repair service. Or set a subject of your own.

YOURS ALONE, OR ON EVERY DEVICE
Without an account everything stays on the phone. Sign in with an email and your levels, conversations, tasks, copybook and listening scores follow you to every device with that account.

ALSO
Your name and a chosen avatar or your own photo · light and dark themes · three text sizes · an archive of finished conversations · iPhone and iPad.

Madame Poly is a practice partner, not a replacement for a teacher or a structured course. A voice tutor cannot work offline: the audio of your turn goes to OpenAI for recognition and the text to Anthropic for the reply. Nothing else is collected — no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
